# Plant seals: official California symbols on cards, 2026-10-28

Eric's request: a "seal" on the Purple needlegrass card, because it is California's state grass.

## The fact (verified 2026-10-28)
- **California Government Code §425.10:** "Purple needlegrass, or Nassella pulchra, is the official State Grass." https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=425.10
- **Enacted by SB 1226 (Machado), Stats. 2004, ch. 243**, approved by the Governor and filed with the Secretary of State on Aug 23, 2004, effective Jan 1, 2005. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=200320040SB1226
- **Correction:** the bill was SB 1226, not AB 2860.
- The statute uses the name *Nassella pulchra*; the feed and Jepson use *Stipa pulchra*. The seal matches both names (`IENN_SEAL_ALIAS`).

## Design
- **Look:** a small scalloped rosette in lilac, on a cream disc with a sage ring. In the middle is a simple grass tuft (sage) with two arching awned seed heads (deep lilac #6e5585). No red.
- **Text:** "CALIFORNIA" curved over the top and "STATE GRASS" curved along the bottom (photo placement), or one line, "California State Grass" (body placement).
- **Not the Great Seal of California**, which has restricted use: no bear, no Minerva, no "Great Seal" wording, no state coat of arms. It's a generic rosette.
- **Built in code:** an inline SVG made by `iennSealEl()` in `data/plant-seals.js`, not a raster image. The `role="img"` and `aria-label` say "California State Grass".

## Data
- `data/plant-seals.js`: `IENN_SEALS` is keyed by exact botanical name (first two words, case-insensitive), with `{ label, arc:[top,bottom], mark, src }`. `IENN_SEAL_ALIAS` maps synonyms.
- **Each entry picks its own center art** with `mark`, from `IENN_SEAL_MARKS`: `grass` (a tuft with awned seed heads) or `poppy` (a cup of 4 petals on a stem).

## Placement (setting `SEAL_PLACEMENT` in `shop-settings.js`; preview with `?seal=photo` or `?seal=body`)
- **body (default, recommended):**
  - A 28px rosette plus "California State Grass" on one line, under the botanical name.
  - It's off the photo, so the price (top-left), badge (top-right), life-form ribbon (bottom-left) and credit (bottom-right) are untouched, and Eric's 3-overlay limit on the photo still holds.
  - The full text is easy to read.
- **photo:**
  - A 60px round seal on the right side of the photo, above the credit line.
  - No collisions were measured, even with a price and "Staff pick" badge forced on.
  - It looks more like a seal, but the curved text is small (about 5px caps at 390px wide), and it makes a 4th overlay when price, badge and ribbon are all showing.
- **Both:** the seal is never copied to the clean-photo panel or the basics panel (checked: 0 seals in `.pane-clean` / `.pane-basics`).

## California poppy = California State Flower (added 2026-10-28, Eric)
- **Cal. Gov. Code §421:** "The golden poppy (Eschscholzia californica) is the official State Flower. April 6 of each year is hereby designated California Poppy Day." https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=421 (read on leginfo directly on 2026-10-28).
- **Entry:** `IENN_SEALS["Eschscholzia californica"]`, label "California State Flower", `mark:"poppy"`. Same under-name placement and same rules as the grass seal.
- **Art:** a side view of 4 cup-shaped petals in the site's honey gold (`--honey` #e8b04b with `--honey-edge`; the darker back petal is #d99a36 and the lighter front petal #f1c46a), on a sage stem with a fine leaf. Cream disc, sage ring, lilac rosette edge. No red.
- **Matilija poppy** (*Romneya coulteri*) is a different plant and does not get the seal. Checked: no seal on its card.

## Check of the live feed against California's official state symbols (2026-10-28)
Source: Cal. Gov. Code, Title 1, Division 2, Chapter 2, "State Flag and Emblems" (§§420–429.9).
- leginfo's chapter view was behind a bot check, so the full list of sections was read from the California.Public.Law mirror of leginfo: https://california.public.law/codes/government_code_section_421 and the linked sections.
- §421 and §425.10 were also read on leginfo itself today.

**Every plant or plant-like symbol in that chapter:**

| Section | Symbol | Species | In the IENN feed (33 plants)? |
|---|---|---|---|
| §421 | State Flower | golden poppy, *Eschscholzia californica* | **Yes**: California poppy → seal added |
| §425.10 | State Grass | purple needlegrass, *Nassella pulchra* (= *Stipa pulchra*) | **Yes**: Purple needlegrass → seal (PR #52) |
| §425.16 | State Shrub | bigberry manzanita, *Arctostaphylos glauca* | No |
| §422 | State Tree | California redwood, *Sequoia sempervirens*, *Sequoia gigantea* (= *Sequoiadendron giganteum*) | No |
| §424.6 | State Lichen | lace lichen, *Ramalina menziesii* | No (not a nursery plant) |
| §425.11 | State Mushroom | California golden chanterelle, *Cantharellus californicus* | No (fungus) |
| §422.3 | State Nut | almond (*Prunus dulcis*), walnut, pistachio, pecan | No (not CA natives, not in the catalog) |

The other sections cover animals, the soil, rocks and minerals, colors, songs, places and so on, and none of them are plants. **Possible future seal:** if bigberry manzanita (*Arctostaphylos glauca*) is ever added to the Catalog, it could get "California State Shrub" (§425.16) with a new shrub mark.
