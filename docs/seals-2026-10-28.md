# Plant seals: official California symbols on cards, 2026-10-28

Eric's request: a "seal" on the Purple needlegrass card, because it is California's state grass.

## The fact (verified 2026-10-28)
- **California Government Code §425.10:** "Purple needlegrass, or Nassella pulchra, is the official State Grass." https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=425.10
- **Enacted by SB 1226 (Machado), Stats. 2004, ch. 243**, approved by the Governor and filed with the Secretary of State on Aug 23, 2004, effective Jan 1, 2005. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=200320040SB1226
- **Correction:** the bill was SB 1226, not AB 2860.
- The statute uses the name *Nassella pulchra*; the feed and Jepson use *Stipa pulchra*. The seal matches both names (`IENN_SEAL_ALIAS`).

## Design
- **Look:** a small scalloped rosette in lilac, on a cream disc with a sage ring. In the middle is a simple grass tuft (sage) with two arching awned seed heads (deep lilac #6e5585). No red.
- **Text:** "CALIFORNIA" curved over the top and "STATE GRASS" curved along the bottom (photo placement), or one line, "California State Grass" (body placement).
- **Not the Great Seal of California**, which has restricted use: no bear, no Minerva, no "Great Seal" wording, no state coat of arms. It's a generic rosette.
- **Built in code:** an inline SVG made by `iennSealEl()` in `data/plant-seals.js`, not a raster image. The `role="img"` and `aria-label` say "California State Grass".

## Data
- `data/plant-seals.js`: `IENN_SEALS` is keyed by exact botanical name (first two words, case-insensitive), with `{ label, arc:[top,bottom], mark, src }`. `IENN_SEAL_ALIAS` maps synonyms.
- **Candidate, not added:** California poppy (*Eschscholzia californica*) is the official State Flower, per Gov. Code §421 (verified 2026-10-28): https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=421. Adding it would need a flower `mark` (the current art is grass only) and Eric's OK.

## Placement (setting `SEAL_PLACEMENT` in `shop-settings.js`; preview with `?seal=photo` or `?seal=body`)
- **body (default, recommended):**
  - A 28px rosette plus "California State Grass" on one line, under the botanical name.
  - It's off the photo, so the price (top-left), badge (top-right), life-form ribbon (bottom-left) and credit (bottom-right) are untouched, and Eric's 3-overlay limit on the photo still holds.
  - The full text is easy to read.
- **photo:**
  - A 60px round seal on the right side of the photo, above the credit line.
  - No collisions were measured, even with a price and "Staff pick" badge forced on.
  - It looks more like a seal, but the curved text is small (about 5px caps at 390px wide), and it makes a 4th overlay when price, badge and ribbon are all showing.
- **Both:** the seal is never copied to the clean-photo panel or the basics panel (checked: 0 seals in `.pane-clean` / `.pane-basics`).
